# ELEMENTOPIA 3D — Chemistry RPG v0.4

## New committed features
- Atom Forge 3D: interactive Three.js atom visualization, orbit controls, drag-and-drop particles and accessible +/− buttons (Na-23: 11 protons, 12 neutrons, 11 electrons).
- Particle Scanner: identity, shell-distribution and alkali-property observations for Li, Na and K.
- Responsive styling for scanner and 3D assembly.
- Existing six missions, XP, quest progression and local save schema retained.

## Important scientific labeling
- Electron rings are educational diagrams, NOT actual electron orbits.
- Li, Na and K reactivity information is for safe conceptual simulations.
